About Me

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Neuroscience at the Technical University of Munich, studying neural computation and cognitive control in the prefrontal cortex. My research focuses on understanding how the brain organizes flexible, goal-directed behavior.

Research

My research combines in vivo electrophysiology, behavioral analysis, and computational modeling to explore how distributed neural populations encode task structures and decision processes.

  • Population coding and representational geometry in PFC
  • Cross-temporal decoding and neural dynamics
  • Comparative neuroscience between primates and rodents

Publications

  2. Wang, X., et al. (2024). Dynamic population geometry of executive control. Neuron.
